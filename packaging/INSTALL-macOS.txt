Veneer
======

Copy the parts you want:

  Veneer.vst3       to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Veneer.component  to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/    (Audio Unit)
  Veneer.app        to  /Applications/                          (stand-alone)

These builds are signed ad hoc, not with an Apple Developer ID, and they are not notarised, so macOS
blocks them the first time. After copying, clear the download flag from whatever you installed:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Veneer.vst3
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Veneer.component
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Veneer.app

Then rescan plug-ins in your host.
